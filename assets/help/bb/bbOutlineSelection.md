# Outline Selection

Selection effects follow the marching ants around the selected area. Outline Selection draws along that boundary.

Without a selection, they use the canvas edges. Rectangular selections work, while curved, hand-drawn and magic-wand selections often produce more useful contours.

No transparency is required. A selection is enough to mark the part of an opaque image you want to change.

For example, select a red letter with the magic wand. Outline, bevel, feather and inner-shadow treatments can all follow that letter without deleting its background first.

For questions about the original effects, visit the [Paint.NET forum](https://forums.getpaint.net/).
