# Vignette+

Vignette+ darkens or colors the edges of a picture using a vignette based on Direct2D's effect.

Choose the vignette color to suit the image. This gives you a color choice beyond Paint.NET's built-in Vignette.

Radius controls the size of the clear central region. Strength controls how opaque the surrounding vignette becomes.

The edge develops gradually with a smooth, sine-shaped falloff rather than a hard border. For example, a dark vignette can guide attention toward a portrait's center without drawing an obvious frame.

For questions about the original effects, visit the [Paint.NET forum](https://forums.getpaint.net/).
