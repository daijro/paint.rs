# Inner Shadow

Selection effects work along the selection outline, shown by the marching ants. Inner Shadow places its shading inside that boundary.

With no selection, the canvas edges become the boundary. A rectangle works, but curved, hand-drawn or magic-wand selections can show more interesting contours.

Transparency is unnecessary. The selection identifies what to change even on an opaque image.

For example, select a red letter with the magic wand. Inner Shadow gives its inside edge a recessed appearance; other Selection effects can bevel, outline or soften the same selected shape.

For questions about the original effects, visit the [Paint.NET forum](https://forums.getpaint.net/).
