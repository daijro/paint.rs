# Switch Alpha to Gray

Switch Alpha to Gray makes an opaque grayscale mask from a layer's transparency. You can edit that mask and use it later with Apply Mask.

A partly transparent letter becomes a gray silhouette: opaque parts are white, transparent parts are black, and intermediate alpha becomes intermediate gray.

Work on a copy because the conversion discards the layer's original colors.

Invert mask reverses black and white when the mask needs the opposite meaning.

After conversion, paint on the mask to reveal, hide or soften selected areas before applying it to another layer.

For questions about the original effects, visit the [Paint.NET forum](https://forums.getpaint.net/).
