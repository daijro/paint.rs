# Apply Mask

A layer mask controls which parts of an image remain visible. Keep the mask on a separate layer just above the image, with the mask layer's visibility off when you do not need to view it.

Check the current layer shown in the dialog, then choose the mask layer. Select alpha when its transparency defines the mask, or shades of gray when its brightness does. Use Invert mask if the visible and hidden areas are reversed.

For example, white can reveal a subject, black can hide its background, and gray can leave a partly transparent transition. A mask made from transparency provides the same kind of control through its alpha channel.

For questions about the original effects, visit the [Paint.NET forum](https://forums.getpaint.net/).
