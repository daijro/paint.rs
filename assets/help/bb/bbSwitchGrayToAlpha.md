# Switch Gray to Alpha

Switch Gray to Alpha separates dark line art from a white background. It removes the white paper and turns gray edges into partly transparent black, preserving their appearance when placed over white.

Put a coloring layer beneath the extracted lines so the drawing stays visible while you paint underneath it.

Use the Black variant when your drawing has light lines on a dark background. It removes black and leaves white lines instead.

For example, a scanned pencil outline can become transparent between strokes, while soft gray marks become translucent lines.

For questions about the original effects, visit the [Paint.NET forum](https://forums.getpaint.net/).
