# Seam Carving - Vertical

Seam carving finds a path through the least-changing parts of a photo, removes that seam, and closes the gap. The two menu items choose the seam direction.

Natural photographs are usually better candidates than lettering or rigid patterns. Clear the selection with Ctrl+D before running it.

Amount to cut chooses how many seams to remove, from 0 to 200; the default is one. Use Ctrl+F to repeat, checking whether important shapes still look right.

To discourage the seam from crossing a subject, lower that area's alpha slightly with Transparency. Restore its opacity afterward. Keep a copy so you can compare the original proportions.

For questions about the original effects, visit the [Paint.NET forum](https://forums.getpaint.net/).
