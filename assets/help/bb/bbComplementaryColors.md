# Complementary Colors

Put your main color in the primary color slot before opening the effect. You can also change it inside the dialog.

Choose a two-color scheme for a complementary pair, or a three-color scheme for split complementary colors.

The choices follow an RYB artist's wheel. Hue positions are numbered around that wheel, so opposite positions identify complementary colors.

A gray primary has no color direction and produces other grays.

RYB reflects traditional paint relationships rather than the RGB wheel used for light. This makes the schemes closer to familiar artist's color combinations.

The results resemble the Complementary and Split Complementary modes found in online color calculators.

For questions about the original effects, visit the [Paint.NET forum](https://forums.getpaint.net/).
