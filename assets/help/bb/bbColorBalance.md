# Color Balance

Use Color Balance to remove an unwanted color cast, such as the warm tint in an indoor photograph taken without flash.

This adjustment changes RGB channels directly. It can tint black, white and gray areas, which behave differently under Hue / Saturation.

The three axes run from Cyan to Red, Magenta to Green, and Yellow to Blue. Move toward the color you want to add, or toward its opposite to reduce it.

Each positive slider adds its amount to one primary channel and removes half that amount from each of the other two. For example, moving toward Red raises red while lowering green and blue. Finish with Brightness and Contrast as needed.

For questions about the original effects, visit the [Paint.NET forum](https://forums.getpaint.net/).
