# Black and White+

Choose a grayscale conversion to emphasize different parts of a photograph. Paint.NET Method, the default, uses 0.299R + 0.587G + 0.114B. Luminosity Method uses 0.2126R + 0.7152G + 0.0722B. These weights make green contribute more than blue.

Average Method gives R, G and B equal shares. Lightness Method averages the largest and smallest channel. Maximum Method uses the largest channel; Minimum Method uses the smallest. Compare a red object against a green background to see how the methods separate colors.

For a stronger result, try Brightness at -10 and Contrast at +10, then adjust for your image.

Blend the grayscale result with a chosen color for a tint. Swap order reverses the two blend inputs. Modes such as Multiply produce the same result in either order.

For questions about the original effects, visit the [Paint.NET forum](https://forums.getpaint.net/).
